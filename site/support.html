Frequently asked questions

How do I use the whistle?

Go to Settings and turn on Show whistle on every screen. A round whistle button then sits just above the tab bar on every main screen. Use Whistle side to put it on the right or the left. Tap it for one short blast, or press and hold for a longer blast (up to about 3 seconds). Any music playing dips under the whistle and comes back after. You can change its color in Settings > Color Themes > Whistle color.

Will the cues keep playing if my screen locks?

Yes. Tabata and Circuit keep the screen on while a workout runs. If you lock the phone anyway, every tone and spoken cue keeps playing while the clock is counting, with your music lowered under it. In-app cues play even when the Ring/Silent switch is set to silent. Pause or stop the workout and the app goes quiet.

For timers on the Timers tab: on iOS 26 and later, a finished timer rings like an alarm on the lock screen, with a slide to stop. On earlier versions of iOS it arrives as a notification, so allow notifications when the app asks (that notification follows the Ring/Silent switch).

How do I use Apple Music?

You can play music from any music app, such as Apple Music, Spotify or YouTube Music. Total Timer PE lowers it during cues and tones and then brings it back. If you subscribe to Apple Music, you can also connect it under Settings > Apple Music Options to play and control your music from inside the app. Music features are free. Apple Music access is used only to play and control music on your device.

How do I save my timers?

In Tabata, set your work, rest and rounds, then tap Save. In Circuit, build your exercise list, then tap Save Circuit. Find them again under Settings > Saved Tabata and Saved Circuits. Press and hold a saved item to rename it, move it to a folder, duplicate it or delete it. On the Timers tab, tap New Timer; your timers stay in the list, and you can press and hold a timer to open its menu or drag it to reorder.

Everything you save stays on your device. Deleting the app deletes it, and we cannot restore it.
