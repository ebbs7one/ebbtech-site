About the company

Built under Ebbtech LLC

Ebbtech LLC is the legal business entity behind Total Timer. The company is focused on building simple, dependable mobile software with a clean interface and clear real-world use cases.

The initial product focus is a timer app that supports interval training, teaching workflows, countdown timers, stopwatch functions, and structured routines for coaches, physical educators, and fitness users.

Product

Total Timer

Total Timer is a multi-purpose timing app designed to combine interval timing, standard timers, lesson support, and workout flow into one mobile experience.

Tabata and interval timing

Set work, rest, and rounds for repeatable interval sessions with large on-screen timing displays.

Standard timers

Create and manage practical countdown timers for workouts, classroom activity blocks, and daily routines.

Stopwatch support

Switch into stopwatch mode for open-ended tracking, pacing, and live activity timing.

Lesson and workout organization

Organize saved content and categories so training sessions and lesson flows can be reused efficiently.

Audio and playback controls

Use reminders, tones, and music-control features to support rhythm, transitions, and focus during sessions.

Designed for real use

Total Timer is being built with practical use in mind for coaches, teachers, and fitness-focused users.
