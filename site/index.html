About the company

Built under Ebbtech LLC

Ebbtech LLC is the legal business entity behind Total Timer PE. The company is focused on building simple, dependable mobile software with a clean interface and clear real-world use cases.

Its first product is Total Timer PE, a free timer app for PE teachers, coaches and fitness users, with interval training, circuits, a stopwatch, countdown timers and a whistle in one place.

Product

Total Timer PE

Total Timer PE is a free iPhone app that puts the timing tools a PE class or a workout needs in one place. No account, no ads and no tracking.

Tabata

Set work, rest and rounds, then follow a large on-screen clock with tones and spoken reminders at each change.

Circuit

Build your own exercise list with a time for each one. The app calls out every exercise by name, with a 3-2-1 lead-in, across as many sets as you choose.

Stopwatch

Count up with laps and splits for runs, relays and anything open-ended.

Timers

Run several named countdown timers at once for stations, warm-ups and water breaks. On iOS 26 and later a finished timer rings on the lock screen like an alarm.

The whistle and sounds

Keep a whistle button on every screen: tap for a short blast, hold for a long one. Choose from tones and spoken cues that keep playing with the phone locked.

Color themes and Apple Music

Pick a color theme or make your own with a background picture. Play music from any app, or connect Apple Music; the app lowers it during cues and brings it back.

Lessons and fitness testing are coming to Total Timer PE Premium.
