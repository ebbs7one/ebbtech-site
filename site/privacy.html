Privacy Policy: Total Timer PE

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Total Timer PE is published by Ebbtech LLC. It is a timer app for PE teachers and coaches. This policy explains what the app does with information.

The short version: Total Timer PE collects no personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device and is never sent to Ebbtech LLC or anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. Total Timer PE collects no personal data of any kind.

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No analytics.
  • No advertising.
  • No tracking.

The app does not send any information to Ebbtech LLC or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter stays on your device, inside the app, and is never sent anywhere. That includes:

  • your timers, Tabata settings and circuits, including any you save;
  • your sound, whistle and color theme choices;
  • any background picture you choose. The app keeps its own copy of that picture on your device and never uploads it.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. Ebbtech LLC cannot see, recover or restore any of it, because none of it ever leaves your device.

Permissions the app may ask for

Total Timer PE asks only when you use the feature that needs it. It works without any of them.

  • Notifications: so a timer can alert you when it ends while the app is closed or your device is locked.
  • Alarms (iOS 26 and later): so a finished timer rings like an alarm, with a slide to stop, on the lock screen.
  • Apple Music: used only to play and control music on your device, and only if you choose to connect it. The app does not use your listening history or library for anything else, and nothing about your music is sent anywhere.
  • Photos: used only to set background pictures, and only when you choose one. You pick a photo in Apple’s photo picker, and the app receives only that photo. Background pictures stay on your device.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: only when you tap Speak to say an exercise name in the Circuit editor. What you say is turned into text on your device to fill in the exercise. It is not stored or sent.

Music from other apps

You can play music from any music app while using Total Timer PE. The app briefly lowers that music during its cues, then brings it back. It does not see what you are playing.

Children

Total Timer PE is designed for teachers and coaches. No data is collected from children or from anyone else.

If you email us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
